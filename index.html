About the collection

Light. Place. Perspective.

Light & Form is an interactive circular rotunda exhibition exploring everyday life, urban geometry, and the quiet relationship between people and architectural space.

Place any .jpg, .webp, or .png files inside the assets/ folder (such as photo-01.webp through photo-36.jpg) and each frame automatically adjusts its width and height to match the image’s native ratio without cropping.
